HOW TO RIDE
Scroll wheel — lean. Scroll one way to lean back, the other to lean forward. Tune it in Settings.
Side button — quick restart to the start of the track.
GAS / BRAKE — the two on-screen pedals. In the air, GAS pitches you back and BRAKE pitches you forward.
Faults — land on your head and you're back at the last checkpoint, clock still running. Each fault adds +5s to your medal time.
Bunny hop — flick lean back then forward fast, double-tap GAS, or press K. Clears logs, pipes and gaps.
Wheelie — hold lean back on the gas. Pivot turn — brake + lean with the front wheel planted.
Bolts & crates — medals pay bolts (⚙). Spend ⚙40 on a crate in the Garage for random cosmetics; EPIC+ is guaranteed within 12 opens.
Ghost — your best medal run races beside you as a translucent ghost (Settings).
Daily Climb — one seeded hill per day; set the best you can, medals pay fresh bolts daily.
Remix — Settings toggle: career tracks shuffle their logs and barrels every run, and scatter bonus ramps and floating platform lines over the flat stretches. Pays bolts, never touches your records.
Lava crosses — on the late tracks, ride the ramp and hop the floating platforms across the lava. Touching the lava is a fault, so commit to the line.
Achievements — 8 trophies in the Garage pay bonus bolts.
Desktop keys — W/↑ gas, S/↓ brake, A/D or ←/→ lean, R restart, C checkpoint restart, K hop, Esc pause.
Tip: ease off the gas before a drop, and lean back early on climbs — momentum is everything.
